## EventPal

<img width="600" alt="Screenshot 2024-08-21 at 5 15 39 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a750ebf7-de10-4d45-bff5-088b375c1f93">

EventPal is an intelligent Slack automation platform that streamlines professional networking and business outreach workflows. Built with Python and integrated with the Slack API, EventPal leverages OCR and large language models (LLMs) to automatically extract structured contact information from business cards, generate personalized introduction emails, and reduce manual administrative effort.
